Delete Your BowPress Account

Last updated: May 26, 2026

BowPress is developed by Andrew Nguyen. This page explains how to delete your BowPress account and the data associated with it.

How to delete your account from inside the app

  1. Open the BowPress app on your iPhone or Android device.
  2. Sign in if you are not already signed in.
  3. Tap the Settings tab.
  4. Tap Account.
  5. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

Your account and the data described below are removed from our systems within 30 days of the request.

If you cannot access the app

Email bowpresssupport@gmail.com from the address tied to your BowPress account with the subject "Delete my account". We will verify ownership of the account and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account record (user ID, email address, display name).
  • All bow configurations, arrow setups, and equipment you created.
  • All shooting sessions, arrow plot data, and session notes.
  • All sight-mark records and personal calibration data.
  • Push-notification device tokens registered to your account.
  • Subscription state held on our servers (your subscription itself is managed by Apple or Google and must be canceled separately if you no longer want to be charged — see below).

What is retained, and for how long

  • Anonymized aggregate analytics — e.g., total counts of sessions logged across the user base, with no link back to you — may be retained indefinitely.
  • Server logs containing request metadata (IP address, timestamps) are retained for up to 30 days for security and abuse-prevention purposes, then deleted.
  • Billing records required by tax or accounting law are retained for the period required by the relevant jurisdiction (typically 7 years).

Canceling your subscription

Deleting your BowPress account does not cancel an active App Store or Google Play subscription, because subscriptions are managed by Apple and Google, not by us. To cancel:

  • iOS: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → BowPress → Cancel.
  • Android: Google Play Store → profile menu → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → BowPress → Cancel.

Contact
